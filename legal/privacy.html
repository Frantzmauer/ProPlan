SixMo Privacy Policy

Draft of 10 November 2026 · effective from: [publication date] · In case of discrepancies, the Polish version prevails.

In short: your data is used only to run your plan. We don't sell it, show ads or use tracking tools. We process health data only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Marek Frankiewicz (“we”). For anything about your data, write to contact@sixmo.app.

2. What data we process

  • Account data: account name, first name (optional), language, plan (Free / Premium), last connection date, AI usage counters.
  • Profile data: age, height, weight, sex, activity level, calorie goal, plan start date.
  • Health data (a special category of data, Art. 9 GDPR): weight and body measurements, meal and calorie logs, workouts and wellbeing (sleep, soreness, energy), and — if you turn the feature on — menstrual cycle data and symptoms.
  • Content you send: questions to the AI Coach and its answers, meal descriptions and photos, frames from exercise videos with movement measurements, barcode numbers.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser information, which reach the hosting and service providers (section 5) with every connection.

3. Where your data is

  • On your device — everything you enter is saved in your browser's or phone app's storage, so the App works offline.
  • On the App's server — with sync on, a copy goes to the server. This is needed so you can use several devices, keep your history when you change or lose your phone, let the AI Coach analyse your progress, and provide the paid Premium service. Cycle data stays on your device by default and goes to the server only if you turn that on.
  • Videos never leave your phone — only a few frames and measurements are sent for analysis. Meal photos are sent for calorie estimation and are not stored on the server.

4. Purposes and legal bases

PurposeLegal basis (GDPR)
Providing the service: account, plan, logging, sync, statisticsArt. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract
Processing health data (measurements, meals, wellbeing, cycle) and sharing it with the AI CoachArt. 9(2)(a) — your explicit consent (you can withdraw it at any time; this doesn't affect earlier processing, but features based on this data will stop working)
Payments and accounting (once Premium launches)Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) — contract and tax obligations
Security, preventing abuse (limits), handling complaints and claimsArt. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal effects. The AI Coach's suggestions require your approval.

5. Who we share data with

We use providers that process data on our behalf (processors):

  • Google (Google Apps Script and Google Drive) — the App's server and storage of synced data.
  • Anthropic (the Claude model) — the AI Coach, calorie estimates and form checks. We send your question with the necessary context from your data, a meal photo or video frames. Anthropic does not use data sent via its API to train models and keeps it for a limited time under its terms.
  • GitHub (hosting the App's website) — receives your IP address when the App's files are downloaded. Fonts, the barcode scanner and body detection are part of the App and don't connect to external servers.
  • Open Food Facts — when you scan a barcode, we send only the barcode number.
  • Once payments launch — the payment provider or app store. [to be completed]

Some providers are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the European Commission's decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses. [To be verified: data processing agreements with providers — see note in docs/PRAWNE.md.] We don't sell data or share it with advertisers.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and synced data — while you use the App; after account deletion we remove it from the server within 30 days (backups — up to 90 days).
  • Accounts inactive for 24 months may be deleted after prior notice.
  • Billing data — for the period required by tax law (usually 5 years).
  • Data on your device — until you delete it (More → Your data → Delete data from this device) or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, restrict processing, data portability (download a JSON file: More → Your data → Download my data), object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to contact@sixmo.app — we will reply within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your country.

8. Device storage and cookies

The App stores data in your browser's storage (localStorage) only so that it works — your logs and settings are kept there. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies and don't track you across other services. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you request, so it doesn't require separate consent.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each account has its own access key and account data is kept separate. Only the administrator has administrative access. No system is fully immune to attacks — if a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk, we will inform you and the supervisory authority as required by the GDPR.

10. Children

The App is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from people under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We will announce significant changes in the App. If a change affects processing based on consent, we will ask for your consent again.
