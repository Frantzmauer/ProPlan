How to delete your SixMo account

In the app

  1. Open SixMo and go to More → Your data.
  2. Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm. An internet connection is required.

Without the app

Email contact@sixmo.app asking to delete your account and give your account name. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • your account, plan and AI usage counters — immediately;
  • synced data (workouts, meals, weight, measurements, cycle, AI Coach chats) — moved to the bin immediately and permanently removed after 30 days;
  • data on your device — the app clears it after the account is deleted.

Billing data (once payments launch) is kept for the period required by tax law. Details: Privacy Policy.
